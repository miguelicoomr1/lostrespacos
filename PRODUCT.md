# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a full-stack React/Vinext application deployed as a Site, selected for a fast public experience and protected server-side reservation logic.

## Users

Visitors to Portmán and local diners choosing a table, consulting the menu or contacting Los Tres Pacos. Staff administer reservations, menu items and gallery content from a protected area.

## Product Purpose

Los Tres Pacos needs its own modern digital home: an editorial restaurant website that makes reserving, calling and reading the menu immediate, while preserving its local and approachable character.

## Positioning

A café-bar in Portmán with traditional Murcian cooking, rice dishes, seafood, tapas and a terrace; the site combines that specific local hospitality with a contemporary, premium digital presentation.

## Operating Context

Reservations offer Salón interior, Salón exterior and Terraza. Reservation seating is fixed at 30-minute intervals: lunch 13:00–15:30 and dinner 19:30–22:30, with a duration of 90 minutes. The public contact telephone is 968 54 84 98.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes: home, nosotros, carta, reservas, galería, contacto and legal pages.
- A reservation is checked server-side for business hours, closed dates, seating zone, capacity and overlap before it is created.
- `/admin` requires real authentication and lets staff manage reservations, menu, gallery and configuration.
- The supplied menu images are the source of truth for the transcribed menu and prices; no ingredients or prices may be inferred.
- Legal identity, reservation inbox, zone capacities, final social links and current general opening hours remain to be supplied before production launch.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Los Tres Pacos, Portmán. The supplied official Facebook page is https://www.facebook.com/lostrespacos. Use the provided black "LOS TRES PACOS · CAFÉ · BAR" logo as the principal lock-up. The illustrated Portmán mark supplied by the user is a secondary asset. The brief requires an original, minimalist, Mediterranean, editorial and photographic identity; Scabetti is only a quality reference and must not be copied.

## Product Principles

- Make booking, calling and the menu faster than navigation.
- Lead with factual local identity and provided photography, never generic restaurant claims.
- Keep the visual system quiet enough for food, place and hospitality to lead.
- Treat availability, personal data and administration as server-side concerns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA where reasonably applicable, with semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, labelled controls, touch-friendly targets and reduced-motion support.
